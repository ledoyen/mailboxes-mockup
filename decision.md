# Mailbox placement: decisions per prototype

234 boxes to place, per column: A 31, B 30, C 40, D 30, E 33, F 40, G 30. Halls: AB serves A+B (61), C (40), D (30), E (33), F (40) and G (30) serve one column each.

## Rules applied

- Wall-mounted only, in the 0.90–1.50 m band (0.60 m tall). Rows per wall: standard 2 (52 cm), semi-vertical 2 (60 cm), semi-horizontal 5 (55 cm).
- A box goes on bare wall only. Excluded: doors, glass doors, loge window, radiators, planters (bacs), mirrors, interphones/digicode, EDF/gas cabinets, lift, stairs. The radiator marked "à supprimer" in hall E is treated as free wall.
- 10 cm kept free at each end of a wall run.
- Radiators: at least 30 cm between any box or bloc and a radiator, measured in plan from the radiator face (depth 12 cm), including a radiator on the neighbouring wall.
- Corners: boxes of two walls are at least 30 cm apart (so every front stays reachable), never overlap, and none overlaps a fixture of the neighbouring wall at its height (planter 50 cm deep, cabinet, lift, interphone, loge, mirror, door). A box or bloc whose position would overlap is moved along its wall to the first free position.
- Collective blocs (B20, C30) are 1.45 m tall: hung on the same bare wall runs with their top at 1.50 m (0.05–1.50 m), one row per wall, so the 0.90–1.50 m band does not apply to them. Per hall, the B20/C30 mix covers the hall's demand with the least wall width (spare boxes allowed); blocs are hung longest first in the tightest run that fits. A hall that cannot hang its blocs sends the rest to the nearest hall with free wall, as for the other prototypes.
- Whole columns of boxes are filled one after another, longest wall run first. Boxes of the hall's own columns first; extras of a full hall go to the nearest hall that still has room (distance measured on the global plan). Nearest order: AB → D, E, C, F, G; C → F, G, E, AB, D; D → E, AB, F, C, G; E → F, D, C, G, AB; F → G, E, C, D, AB; G → F, C, E, D, AB.
- Positions below: wall side is the sheet orientation (top of the plan = N), measured in cm from the north-west corner (N/S walls from the west end, E/W walls from the north end).

## Concerns

- **Plan readings are estimates.** The plans are hand sketches and several dimensions are illegible. Wall segments live in `mockup/halls.mjs`; correcting a length there recomputes everything. E, G and F are the most sensitive (a 30 cm error changes a column).
- **Standard and semi-vertical overflow into several halls.** Standard sends 31 boxes (E into C and D, F into C, G into AB and C); semi-vertical sends 35, including 17 into AB, which is far from G and F on the global plan. Only the semi-horizontal prototype avoids shared halls.
- **Hall C and AB are the main hosts** (standard: C takes 12 boxes, AB 11; semi-vertical: AB takes 25, C 8), D takes some of E's overflow. Check that residents of E, F and G accept collecting their mail in another hall.
- **Depth into the hall:** standard protrudes 34 cm, semi-horizontal 27.5 cm, semi-vertical 15.7 cm. Hall E and G are 2.85 m wide with a door swing on one side, so check egress width and accessibility (PMR) rules before choosing standard.
- **Column grouping:** boxes are not yet grouped per column/floor inside a hall; only counts and walls are decided. Per-column counts are in `BOXES` in `mockup/halls.mjs`.
- **Collective blocs are 1.45 m tall and 25 cm deep**, hung with their top at 1.50 m: the lowest row is at 0.05 m, which is hard to reach and to read, and the 0.90–1.50 m reach band is not respected. Confirm this is acceptable, or the blocs would have to start at 0.90 m (top at 2.35 m).
- **Spare boxes in blocs (16)** come from the bloc sizes (20 and 30); AB and E lose 9 and 7 boxes of wall they could not use otherwise.
- **Benoit leaves 2 boxes unplaced** (A needs 31 for a C30 of 30, E needs 33 for 2 × B16 = 32). Either two more boxes elsewhere, or A and E change bloc (E: B20 + B16 = 36, 3 spare).
- **Radiator gap (30 cm) and corners:** they cost wall in AB (north-wall blocs start 42 cm from the corner) and shrink capacities, mostly for the small boxes: standard AB 130 → 118, C 60 → 56, D 40 → 38, F 40 → 38, G 18 → 16; semi-vertical AB 122 → 108, F 36 → 34; semi-horizontal AB 225 → 205, G 35 → 30; B20 + C30 AB 480 → 450.
- **Clearances (30 cm from radiators, 30 cm between boxes of two walls) and corners** cost wall and shrink the box capacities: standard AB 130 → 114, C 60 → 52, D 40 → 38, F 40 → 38, G 18 → 14; semi-vertical AB 122 → 104, C 52 → 48, D 36 → 34, F 36 → 34, G 16 → 14; semi-horizontal AB 225 → 190, C 95 → 85, G 35 → 25 (G now sends 5 boxes to F); B20 + C30 AB 480 → 410, G 70 → 60. Blocs keep their own halls. The corner gap keeps every box front reachable: boxes on two walls never touch in a corner.
